FAQ

Does it work offline?

Yes, once the local model is downloaded. Audio is processed on your device. Older versions and optional account features are described separately in the privacy policy.

How does the iPhone keyboard work?

The keyboard replaces the system keyboard with a dictation panel. iOS requires recording to start in the VoiceFlow app. Return to the keyboard to stop recording and insert the text automatically or with a button.

Is VoiceFlow available on the App Store?

Not yet. We are testing the iPhone app. The App Store download will become available after Apple approves the release.
